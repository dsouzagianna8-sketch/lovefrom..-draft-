What goes into the cost?

Your plan has a set service period. The price of any gift depends on what you choose, and it's paid when you set it up, so nobody gets a bill later.

Is this a will?

No. A will plans what happens to your things. Love From plans what happens to your words, voice and little traditions. It isn't inheritance, probate or legal execution.

What if I change my mind?

Edit, re-time or delete anything at any point while you're alive. Drafts are never sent.

Need a little help?

Find out why you received this and what happens if you wait or decline.

Why have I received this?

Sam made this for you while he was alive and asked us to deliver it. His trusted contact confirmed his death and we checked it before anything was sent.

What happens if I wait?

Nothing at all. It stays sealed and safe here for as long as you need, with no deadline and no reminders unless you ask for them. Waiting is not the same as declining.

How do I decline it?

Choose "Decline this letter". Only then does a 30-day window start, so you can still change your mind. After 30 days it's deleted for good. If you're not sure, you don't need to decline: just leave it until you're ready.
